# MOTHER2 -> DOLZORE DIFFERENTIATION GUARDRAILS

Authority: DOLZORE MOTHER2 research issue #26
Purpose: prevent reference research from converging into an identifiable clone.

## 1. Core rule

Research the reason something works, then change its expression, structure and implementation.

A DOLZORE feature fails differentiation if a reasonable player can identify the MOTHER2 source mainly from:
- map shape;
- character silhouette;
- costume/color blocking;
- battle UI;
- exact HP presentation;
- dialogue cadence;
- melody/arrangement;
- enemy concept;
- place sequence;
- iconic prop/event combination.

## 2. Map guardrail

Allowed:
- connected roads;
- civic spines;
- social pockets;
- quiet edges;
- optional anchors;
- changing density.

Forbidden:
- tracing/recreating town geometry;
- matching road bends/landmark placement;
- reproducing exact building clusters;
- copying dungeon route topology.

Required transformation:
Change at least:
- topology;
- landmark function;
- architecture culture;
- traversal mechanic;
- event use;
- audio identity.

## 3. Character guardrail

Allowed:
- large readable head mass;
- clear silhouette;
- few strong color blocks;
- directional walk cycles.

Forbidden:
- recognizable hero/cast silhouettes;
- iconic hats/hair/costume combinations;
- matching palette blocks;
- pixel edits of reference sprites.

Required process:
Start from written DOLZORE role and silhouette thumbnails with reference sprites closed.

## 4. Dialogue guardrail

Allowed:
- concise human lines;
- state-aware variants;
- mundane + strange coexistence;
- repeated-talk escalation;
- post-event reactions.

Forbidden:
- imitating Itoi sentence rhythm;
- rephrasing famous lines;
- "MOTHER-style" joke generator;
- copying unusual wording structures.

Required process:
Write from NPC biography, intent and local situation.

## 5. Humor guardrail

Allowed mechanisms:
- misalignment;
- over-specificity;
- social friction;
- consequence inversion;
- bureaucracy;
- earnest oddity.

Forbidden:
Use of a reference joke's setup/object/target with only renamed nouns.

## 6. Music guardrail

Allowed:
- place-specific music;
- service/vehicle states;
- experimental battle/anomaly sound;
- recurring original motifs;
- stylistic diversity.

Forbidden:
- reference melody;
- chord/melody imitation recognizable as specific track;
- copied bass line;
- samples;
- arrangement clone;
- soundalike intended to evoke one exact track.

Required:
Original composition brief based on emotional/functional tags.

## 7. Battle guardrail

Allowed:
- visible field threats;
- encounter advantage from approach;
- fast resolution of trivial fights;
- temporal pressure in turn-based combat;
- abstract/procedural battle backgrounds.

Forbidden:
- rolling number/drum HP look;
- exact transition swirls;
- copied menu hierarchy/layout;
- copied PSI/effect visual vocabulary;
- exact distortion formulas/patterns.

Required:
Original delayed-consequence mechanic and original shader grammar if these principles are adopted.

## 8. Worldbuilding guardrail

Allowed:
ordinary contemporary life + anomaly.

Forbidden:
rebuilding identifiable sequence of:
suburban town -> reference-like rescue town -> zombie town -> skyscraper city -> resort -> swamp etc. with one-to-one analogues.

Required:
DOLZORE's ZURE phenomenon, cultures, economy, jobs, time layers and life systems must reorganize the experience.

## 9. Enemy guardrail

Allowed:
turn ordinary/social/environmental concepts into threats.

Forbidden:
same ordinary object/creature/joke concept with renamed label and similar silhouette.

Required:
Enemy concept must originate from DOLZORE region, ZURE rule, job/life system, ecology or culture.

## 10. UI guardrail

Allowed:
high contrast;
stable information;
shallow menus;
context interaction.

Forbidden:
black windows with reference-like borders/type/placement;
rolling HP;
matching battle framing.

Required:
DOLZORE UI style guide independent of MOTHER2 screenshots.

## 11. Place-name and narrative guardrail

Do not use numbered-town naming gimmick as a defining world structure.
Do not use eight-melody/sanctuary analogue with renamed terms.
Do not recreate character-party order or iconic scene sequence.

## 12. Review checklist

Before accepting a MOTHER2-inspired feature:
- Can we explain the principle without naming MOTHER2?
- Does the feature derive from DOLZORE fiction?
- Are geometry/art/audio/text original?
- Is at least one core structural dimension changed?
- Would the feature still make sense if the reference game were unknown?
- Does it strengthen existing DOLZORE doctrine rather than replace it?

If "no" to any of these, redesign.
