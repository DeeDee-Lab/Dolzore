# MOTHER2 NARRATIVE / PACING / MEMORY GRAMMAR

Authority: DOLZORE MOTHER2 research issue #26
Purpose: convert macro pacing and memory design into original DOLZORE planning tools.

## 1. Main objective can be simple while local episodes are rich

OFFICIAL_NINTENDO describes a broad journey with companions and a recurring melody-collection objective.

Reusable principle:
A clear macro objective can coexist with episodic local problems that dominate moment-to-moment attention.

DOLZORE:
Macro objective should be explainable in one or two sentences.
Local chapters may have their own genre/tone.

## 2. Journey structure over constant plot escalation

The player travels because the world itself matters.

Area progression can function as:
- home;
- neighboring society;
- local crisis;
- larger city;
- recovery/leisure;
- harsh travel;
- hidden culture;
- internal/psychological space;
- large-scale late world;
- final rupture.

This is a functional pattern, not an exact sequence to copy.

## 3. Repeating macro motif / destination structure

A recurring class of meaningful places gives a long RPG:
- direction;
- anticipation;
- collection/progress feedback;
- opportunity for varied local gameplay.

DOLZORE could use an original recurring world phenomenon tied to ZURE.

Do not use:
- eight sites because MOTHER2 does;
- melody collection;
- sanctuary terminology;
- same progression order.

## 4. Rest arcs are real content

CREATOR_INTERVIEW:
Miura deliberately valued travel pleasure and a comfortable destination before a difficult region.

DOLZORE pacing chart must include:
- threat peaks;
- recovery valleys;
- social/funny intervals;
- mystery intervals;
- quiet reflection.

A graph that only rises in danger will flatten emotionally.

## 5. Episodic genre switching

One reason the journey feels broad is that local arcs can evoke different genres:
- suburban adventure;
- social rescue;
- horror;
- urban satire;
- resort/travel;
- survival/exposure;
- surreal/anomalous;
- introspective;
- cosmic threat.

DOLZORE:
Assign each episode a primary genre and one contrast genre.

Avoid repeating identical "arrive -> dungeon -> boss -> leave" cadence.

## 6. Player-specific memories over uniform spectacle

Creator/retrospective evidence emphasizes that different players remember different fragments.

Design implication:
Not every memorable scene should be mandatory cinematic content.

Distribute MemoryAnchors across:
- dialogue;
- object;
- music;
- location;
- funny inconvenience;
- optional NPC;
- rare condition;
- small visual scene.

## 7. Callback economy

Small early details become stronger when referenced later.

DOLZORE CallbackData:
- source anchor;
- delay interval;
- callback type;
- altered meaning;
- optional/mandatory;
- emotional target.

Do not callback everything; selective recurrence creates meaning.

## 8. Home as emotional comparator

Starting from ordinary domestic life gives later scale meaning.

DOLZORE:
Keep first-town/home state revisitable or at least remembered through:
- messages;
- NPC reactions;
- visual state changes;
- music/motif;
- items.

## 9. Vulnerability breaks power fantasy monotony

Reference protagonist experience includes embarrassment, homesickness-like vulnerability and mundane inconvenience.

DOLZORE:
Power progression should be interrupted by human states.
Use original mechanics tied to:
- fatigue;
- uncertainty;
- social bonds;
- fear;
- longing;
- preparation.

## 10. Tone may turn quickly

Do not telegraph "now the serious chapter begins" with total separation.

A warm/funny space may contain threat.
A harsh space may contain silly residents.
A frightening event may be followed by mundane logistics.

This emotional adjacency is a core atmosphere mechanism.

## 11. Final escalation must cash ordinary-world investment

Abstract/cosmic finale works better after the player has:
- eaten;
- traveled;
- talked;
- rested;
- shopped;
- laughed;
- been embarrassed;
- known residents.

DOLZORE:
Do not rush to mythology before daily-life attachment exists.

## 12. Post-climax return / reflection

Creator evidence values conversations after major conclusion.

DOLZORE:
Plan post-climax return states:
- residents react;
- services reopen/close;
- personal relationships resolve;
- world scars remain;
- optional goodbye scenes.

The finale is not only the boss defeat.

## 13. Narrative data model

EpisodeDefinition:
- episode_id;
- primary_genre;
- contrast_genre;
- stress_curve[];
- locations[];
- mandatory_beats[];
- optional_anchors[];
- callbacks[];
- world_state_changes[];
- recovery_points[];
- audio_states[];
- post_event_state.

## 14. Pacing telemetry for authoring

Original DOLZORE authoring metrics:
- minutes since last safe social interaction;
- minutes since last route choice;
- minutes since last optional interaction;
- encounters per travel interval;
- mandatory dialogue density;
- cutscene/control ratio;
- recovery interval;
- unique sensory changes per episode.

Metrics flag monotony; they are not automatic quality scores.

## 15. First-town application

First town should establish:
- home/ordinary baseline;
- local daily-life functions;
- 3+ memorable residents;
- one small anomaly;
- one local incident;
- one after-incident change;
- one quiet route;
- one social pocket;
- 3 optional memory anchors;
- reason to return.

Do not introduce full cosmology immediately.
