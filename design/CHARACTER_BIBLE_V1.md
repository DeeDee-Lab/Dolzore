# DOLZORE CHARACTER BIBLE v1
Authority: 2026-09-29 JST
Scope: core four characters for the growing DOLZORE RPG

This document is canonical for character writing, art, dialogue, quests and future animation.

The goal is not to imitate MOTHER/EarthBound characters.
The goal is to create four original people who remain recognizable at small pixel scale and who can carry a long-running DOLZORE story.

---

# 1. SORA / ソラ

## Basic role
- Player character / viewpoint character
- Apparent age: around 13
- Gender presentation: intentionally neutral
- Home: recently moved to the edge of DOLZORE Town
- Core theme: curiosity without bravado

## Personality
SORA is not a loud hero.
They notice small things first: a bent sign, a strange sound behind a wall, a shop light still on after closing.

They usually act before making a speech.
When something is strange, the first reaction is often observation rather than fear.

Strengths:
- curious
- patient
- notices patterns
- good listener
- willing to help without needing praise

Weaknesses:
- hesitates when asked to make a big decision
- sometimes follows interesting things too far
- can seem quiet or distant
- avoids asking for help until late

## Speech
Short, plain and concrete.
SORA's dialogue should rarely exceed two short lines.

Examples of voice:
- 「あれ。さっきは なかった。」
- 「音がする。中かな。」
- 「もう少しだけ 見てみよう。」
- 「これは……持って帰っていいのかな。」

Avoid:
- heroic speeches
- fake-poetic lines
- constant jokes
- direct imitation of any existing game protagonist

## Visual identity
Native sprite target: 32×40 px.

Silhouette:
- rounded dark-brown hair
- one upward forelock, slightly off-center
- mint short jacket
- cream diagonal shoulder strap
- coral utility pouch at right hip
- charcoal tapered trousers
- off-white sneakers with one coral pixel accent

Face:
- two dark eye pixels
- tiny nose/shadow pixel
- small neutral mouth
- expression is readable mainly through eyes/eyebrow pixels later

Signature object:
- small cross-body field pouch

Why it matters:
The cream diagonal strap must make SORA recognizable even from behind or when the sprite is moving.

## Likes
- maps with handwritten corrections
- vending machines with unusual drinks
- quiet early mornings
- taking apart broken things if no one minds
- listening to MELO describe sounds

## Dislikes
- people shouting instructions from far away
- being rushed
- sticky hands
- doors that look unlocked but are not

## Fear / tension
SORA dislikes the feeling of not knowing whether a sound came from nearby or very far away.

This becomes useful later for audio-driven exploration.

## Relationships
MELO:
SORA thinks MELO notices sounds that everyone else ignores.
MELO trusts SORA to actually listen.

YUZU:
YUZU gives SORA facts and rumors.
SORA often tests whether those facts are actually true.

PON:
PON pulls SORA into detours.
SORA is usually the one who remembers why they left home in the first place.

## Long-term arc
SORA begins as someone who observes the town.
Over time, the town starts reacting to SORA's choices.
The growth is not "become stronger" first; it is "become someone the town trusts."

## Hidden detail
SORA keeps every useless-looking small object until someone proves it is truly useless.

# 2. MELO / メロ

## Basic role
- MUSIC building resident
- Apparent age: around 14
- Core theme: careful listening

## Personality
MELO is direct, warm and slightly stubborn.
They do not say a song is "good" or "bad" quickly.
They describe what it does to a scene.

Strengths:
- focused
- honest
- sensitive to detail
- dependable
- good at noticing changes in rhythm or mechanical noise

Weaknesses:
- overthinks small differences
- can spend too long choosing
- becomes blunt when tired
- dislikes vague explanations

## Speech
MELO speaks concretely.

Examples:
- 「その音、少しだけ右から聞こえる。」
- 「速い曲じゃなくて、前に進む曲がほしい。」
- 「20秒あれば、合うかどうかは分かるよ。」
- 「同じに聞こえる？ じゃあ、もう一回。」

Avoid:
- mystical music language
- generic "music is life" statements

## Visual identity
Native sprite target: 32×40 px.

Silhouette:
- auburn asymmetric bob
- one longer left-side lock
- square amber hair clip
- dark rose overshirt
- cream inner shirt
- deep plum shorts/skirt-like lower mass over dark leggings
- record-sleeve shoulder bag

Signature object:
- square record-sleeve bag

Palette:
- auburn
- burgundy
- cream
- amber
- deep plum

## Likes
- old buttons with a good click
- speakers that are too heavy
- songs that leave room for dialogue
- late-afternoon light
- organizing records by feeling rather than alphabet

## Dislikes
- autoplay without permission
- clipping/distorted speakers
- people calling every upbeat track "happy"
- tangled cables

## Relationships
SORA:
MELO uses SORA as an honest first listener because SORA does not pretend to understand.

YUZU:
They argue over whether notes should be organized by "fact" or "feeling."

PON:
PON keeps borrowing cables and returning different cables.

## Long-term arc
MELO begins as someone who wants everything to sound exactly right.
Later, they learn that imperfect sounds can carry memories better than perfect ones.

## Future quest hooks
- Silent Jukebox
- Lost record sleeve
- Strange hum under the MUSIC floor
- Evening broadcast experiment

## Hidden detail
MELO can identify several town machines by motor noise alone.

# 3. YUZU / ユズ

## Basic role
- JOURNAL / town notice-board resident
- Apparent age: around 15
- Core theme: collecting facts without losing the human story

## Personality
YUZU likes information, but not for its own sake.
They want to know what a fact changes for someone.

Strengths:
- organized
- observant
- good memory
- skeptical in a healthy way
- good at comparing options

Weaknesses:
- writes too many notes
- can forget to actually make a decision
- sometimes corrects people at the wrong moment
- worries about missing one important detail

## Speech
YUZU is slightly more complete and structured than the others.

Examples:
- 「それ、昨日と値段が違う。」
- 「うわさは二つある。確認できたのは一つ。」
- 「分からないなら、分からないって書けばいい。」
- 「数字だけだと、使いやすいかは分からないよ。」

Avoid:
- corporate analyst voice
- long exposition dumps

## Visual identity
Native sprite target: 32×40 px.

Silhouette:
- dark wavy hair, wider at the lower sides
- mustard cardigan
- off-white collar
- olive long lower silhouette
- narrow dark shoes
- notebook visible at left side

Signature object:
- off-white notebook with one red bookmark pixel

Palette:
- charcoal
- mustard
- olive
- off-white
- muted red accent

## Likes
- receipts
- maps with dates
- secondhand shops
- reading instructions after trying the thing once
- pencils shorter than expected

## Dislikes
- screenshots without dates
- "probably"
- price tags covering important labels
- lost bookmarks

## Relationships
SORA:
YUZU gives SORA information, then quietly waits to see what SORA does with it.

MELO:
Frequent friendly arguments about whether something can be measured.

PON:
YUZU has stopped asking why PON's directions never match the map.

## Long-term arc
YUZU begins by believing that enough information prevents mistakes.
Later they learn that choosing still requires judgment.

## Future quest hooks
- Missing issue of the local paper
- Wrong map coordinates
- Old price tag mystery
- Night notice-board messages

## Hidden detail
YUZU keeps a private page titled "things I was wrong about."

# 4. PON / ポン

## Basic role
- Wandering resident / exploration catalyst
- Apparent age: around 13
- Core theme: motion, detours and accidental discovery

## Personality
PON is cheerful but not hyperactive.
They are always going somewhere, yet the destination changes easily.

Strengths:
- fearless about talking to strangers
- remembers routes by landmarks
- adapts quickly
- finds hidden paths by accident
- can make awkward situations less tense

Weaknesses:
- forgets initial goals
- poor sense of time
- returns borrowed objects late
- confidently gives directions that are only partly correct

## Speech
PON speaks in short bursts and often changes direction mid-thought.

Examples:
- 「駅に行く。……たぶん。」
- 「近道あるよ。近いかは知らないけど。」
- 「あっち、昨日は通れた。」
- 「これ借りたの、メロだっけ。ユズだっけ。」

## Visual identity
Native sprite target: 32×40 px.

Silhouette:
- tousled slate-blue hair
- deep blue short jacket
- vivid orange scarf
- navy trousers
- tan square backpack
- slightly wider walking stance than SORA

Signature objects:
- orange scarf
- box-shaped backpack

Palette:
- slate blue
- navy
- orange
- tan
- warm skin tone

## Likes
- alleys
- buses even when walking is faster
- strange stickers
- rooftops
- snacks with limited-edition packaging

## Dislikes
- standing in line
- closed shortcuts
- explaining exactly where something happened
- clocks without second hands

## Relationships
SORA:
PON treats SORA as the person most likely to say "okay" to a detour.

MELO:
PON borrows equipment without understanding the cable names.

YUZU:
PON gives YUZU rumors. About half become useful leads.

## Long-term arc
PON begins as comic movement in the town.
Later, their wandering becomes important because they know how areas connect better than anyone.

## Future quest hooks
- Shortcut that only exists at certain hours
- Borrowed cable chain
- Lost bus pass
- Rooftop signal
- Detour into the second district

## Hidden detail
PON is actually very good at remembering faces, even when names are forgotten.

# 5. Group dynamics

The four should not feel like four variations of the same friendly NPC.

Core contrast:
- SORA = observe
- MELO = listen
- YUZU = verify
- PON = move

Typical group problem:
MELO notices a sound.
YUZU writes down where and when.
PON knows a strange route to the source.
SORA chooses whether to follow it.

This structure should influence quests.

---

# 6. Dialogue rules

Each named character must eventually have:
- 5 default town lines
- 3 interior lines
- 2 lines after each major quest
- 1 rare line
- 1 weather/time-specific line per supported state

No line should exist just to sound quirky.
Every line should do at least one:
- reveal character;
- reveal world;
- give useful information;
- react to player progress;
- create a memorable small joke.

# 9. Character quality gate

Reject a sprite revision if:
- all four share the same head contour;
- accessories disappear at 1× internal game resolution;
- face reads as a random cluster of pixels;
- front/back silhouettes cannot be distinguished;
- character looks like a placeholder person;
- colors are the only way to tell characters apart.

The player should recognize each person from silhouette + one accessory before reading the name.
