# DOLZORE 3D Town Design Standard V1

Date: 2026-10-01 JST

## Purpose

Convert cross-city FF11 spatial observations into a reusable original DOLZORE 3D authoring standard.

This does not recreate Bastok, San d'Oria, Windurst, or Jeuno. The source cities are measurement/reference inputs only.

A useful DOLZORE design conclusion is that active urban streets should usually expose a person, prop, storefront, sign, entrance, or other interest anchor every roughly 6–10 meters, while quiet edges can stretch toward 16–24 meters.

## V1 DOLZORE area module

- 96m × 72m nominal module
- primary road: 7.2m
- secondary road: 5.4m
- sidewalk: 2.0m
- local vertical change: 1.5–3.0m
- landmark spacing: 30–45m
- social-cluster radius: 3–6m

These are DOLZORE-derived authoring values, not claims about exact FF11 road widths or character scale.
